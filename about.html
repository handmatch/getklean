Our story

Born in the pandemic. Growing nationwide.

Getklean was established at the height of the pandemic as a source of quality home cleaning services and support for businesses.

Today, Getklean Philippines is a premier provider of comprehensive cleaning and air conditioning maintenance services, dedicated to delivering high-quality solutions to residential and commercial clients.

We are expanding nationwide and exploring opportunities to scale up our business, so we can help even more people reclaim their space.
