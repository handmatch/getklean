Housemaid FAQ

Can I book for today?

Yes. Choose “Book now” and pick today or tomorrow. Start times run from 7:00AM to 3:00PM.

What’s the difference between half day and whole day?

Half day is 4 hours of service. Whole day is 8 hours: 7 hours of service plus a 1-hour break.

Can I choose my housemaid?

Yes. You’ll see recommended housemaids near you with their rating and distance. Pick one, save favorites for next time, or let us match you.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before each visit. Flexi members also get a free reschedule once a month.
